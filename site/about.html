About the studio

Memorio Studio LLC

Memorio Studio LLC is an independent software company, organised as a limited liability company in the State of Wyoming, United States. We design, build and publish our own software.

What we make

We build consumer software — the kind a person uses on their own device, on their own time. Our work concerns personal organisation: helping people keep hold of the things they are responsible for. We take a small number of products and carry each one the whole way, from research through design and engineering to release and long-term support.

How the studio is organised

The studio is deliberately small. The people who decide what a product should do are the same people who build it, which keeps decisions close to their consequences and removes the translation losses that come with handing work between teams.

We work in long cycles. A feature that is not ready does not ship, and a date we cannot commit to is not announced. This is slower in the short term and considerably faster over the life of a product.

What we publish

Applications published by Memorio Studio are distributed through established app marketplaces under the studio's name as developer of record. Each carries its own documentation and privacy details describing what it does and what it collects.

Our first product is in development and has not been released. It will be listed on this site when it is available.

Working with us

We are open to conversations about partnerships, licensing and distribution. Enquiries of any kind — business, legal, press or security — are welcome at contact@memoriostudio.com, and they are read by a person.
