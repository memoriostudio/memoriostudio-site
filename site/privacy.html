Privacy policy

Last updated 19 August 2026

This policy explains how Memorio Studio LLC ("Memorio Studio", "we") handles personal information. It covers this website. Each application we publish carries its own privacy details, shown on its App Store listing and inside the app.

The short version

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not serve advertising and we do not use advertising identifiers.
  • This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or third-party trackers.
  • Our applications are designed to keep personal content on the customer's own device wherever the feature allows it.

Information this website collects

Nothing you do not send us. This site is a set of static pages. It has no accounts, no forms, no cookies, no analytics and no embedded third-party content.

Our hosting provider records standard server logs — including IP address, request time and user agent — for the purpose of serving the site and protecting it from abuse. We do not use those logs to build a profile of you.

Information you send us directly

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach. We use it only to answer you, and we keep it no longer than we need for that purpose and for our ordinary business records.

Applications

Our applications are separate from this website and are described by their own privacy details. Where an application is designed to store personal content on the customer's own device, that content is not transmitted to us and we cannot read it. Any exception is stated in that application's own privacy details before the feature is used.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We share information only with service providers who host or operate our systems on our behalf, and only as needed for them to do that work; and where we are legally required to.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, to request its correction or deletion, and to appeal a refusal. Residents of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and other U.S. states with comprehensive privacy laws have these rights under those laws; residents of the United Kingdom and the European Economic Area have them under the UK GDPR and GDPR.

To exercise any of them, email legal@memoriostudio.com. We will respond within the period required by the applicable law. We will not discriminate against you for making a request.

Children

Our website is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through it. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information in our care. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we do not claim otherwise.

International visitors

We operate from the United States. If you contact us from outside the United States, your message will be processed there.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be described here rather than made silently.
