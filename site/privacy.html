Privacy policy

Last updated 19 August 2026

This policy explains how Memorio Studio LLC ("Memorio Studio", "we") handles personal information in connection with this website. Applications we publish are covered by their own privacy details, shown on their App Store listing and inside the application.

What this website collects

This site is a set of static pages. It has no accounts, no forms, no cookies, no analytics and no embedded third-party content. It does not collect personal information from visitors.

Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs — including IP address, request time and user agent — in order to serve the site and protect it from abuse. We do not use those logs to build a profile of you.

Information you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach. We use it to respond to you and to keep ordinary business records, and we retain it no longer than needed for those purposes.

Applications

Applications published by Memorio Studio are separate from this website. Each one describes what it collects, and why, in its own privacy details and on its App Store listing.

How we share information

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We share information with service providers who host or operate our systems on our behalf, only as needed for that work, and where we are required to by law.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, to request its correction or deletion, and to appeal a refusal. Residents of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and other states with comprehensive privacy laws have these rights under those laws; residents of the United Kingdom and the European Economic Area have them under the UK GDPR and GDPR.

To exercise any of them, write to contact@memoriostudio.com. We respond within the period the applicable law requires, and we will not treat you differently for asking.

Children

This website is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through it.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information in our care. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we do not claim otherwise.

Where we operate

We operate from the United States. If you contact us from elsewhere, your message is processed there.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Material changes will be described here rather than made quietly.
