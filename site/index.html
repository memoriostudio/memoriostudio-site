What we build

Personal organisation, without the surveillance

Our work focuses on coordination and personal organisation: software that helps people keep track of what they are responsible for. We design so that personal content stays on the customer's own device wherever the feature allows it.

On-device by default

Personal content is designed to stay on the customer's device wherever the feature allows it. Exceptions are stated before a feature is used, not buried.

No advertising business

We do not sell personal information, we do not serve ads, and we do not use advertising identifiers. Our products are paid, not funded by data.

We describe what our software actually does. Where something is unproven or still in development, we say so rather than implying otherwise.
