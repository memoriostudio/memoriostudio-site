An independent studio, not a services firm.

Memorio Studio LLC designs and publishes its own software.

We work on a small number of products at a time and take them the whole way — research, design, engineering, release and support. That means the people who decide what something should do are the same people who build it.

What we ship carries our name, and we stand behind it.

How we work

A short list, and we hold to it.

Built in house

Design and engineering under one roof, so decisions and consequences stay together.

Verified before announced

We test our work before we describe it. Where something is unfinished, we say so.

Long horizons

We build products meant to be used for years, not campaigns meant to be launched once.

What we build with

A deliberately narrow toolkit, chosen for longevity rather than novelty.

Native, not wrapped

We write directly against the platform — Swift and SwiftUI on Apple devices — so our software behaves the way the system expects rather than approximating it.

Local-first architecture

We design so the work happens on the device that owns the data. It makes software that is fast without a network and durable when there isn't one.

Encrypted storage

Where our software keeps personal records, it keeps them in an encrypted local store with keys held by the device's own secure hardware.

Measured, not asserted

Performance and correctness are held to written budgets and automated checks. A claim we cannot demonstrate is a claim we do not make.

Few dependencies

Every third-party component is a long-term liability. We take on very few, pin what we take, and read what we ship.

Built to be maintained

Software outlives the enthusiasm that produced it. We favour approaches a small team can still support in five years.
