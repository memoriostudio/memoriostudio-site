Memorio Studio is an independent development studio building applications for Apple platforms. Small team, long horizons, one product at a time.

01

The studio

Memorio Studio LLC is a United States limited liability company and the developer of record for the applications it publishes. We are a small, self-directed team — we design, build, test and ship our own work rather than taking it on for others.

We are not an agency and we do not sell services. Everything we release is our own.

DisciplineDesign, engineering and release handled in-house, end to end.

02

How we work

We build for iPhone first, natively, in Swift. We prefer a small number of decisions made carefully to a large number made quickly, and we would rather ship one thing that holds up than several that need explaining.

Our releases are verified before they are announced. When something is unfinished or unproven, we say so — including here.

PlatformiPhone · Swift and SwiftUI · Native, not wrapped.

03

Current work

Our first application is in development and has not been released. When it ships it will be listed here with its App Store page. We do not publish release dates we cannot keep, and we do not run a waiting list.
